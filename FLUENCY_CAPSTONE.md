### The Three-Beat Execution Framework (Week 2 Shape)
Every new piece of work added to this platform follows the verified 3-beat rhythm:

1. **Beat 1: The Problem (Context, Friction & Human Reality)**
   - *What was broken or missing before?*
   - *Focus:* Identify the raw human, operational, or ecological constraint before technology was introduced (e.g., smallholder beekeepers in semi-arid Kenya losing up to 40% of their honey harvest because rural hive absconding and swarming cannot be detected without manual, disruptive box opening).

2. **Beat 2: What I Did (Architecture, Constraints & Deliberate Trade-offs)**
   - *What technical choices were made, and why?*
   - *Focus:* Document engineering decisions, trade-offs, and tool selection with transparency. Explain why one path was chosen over an easier alternative (e.g., opting for an offline USSD/SMS fallback gateway over a smartphone-only web app to guarantee 100% reach for pastoralist farmers with feature phones).

3. **Beat 3: What Came of It (Measurable Impact, Data & Honest Reflection)**
   - *What was the real outcome?*
   - *Focus:* Deliver quantifiable numbers, operational gains, and what was learned (e.g., telemetry latency dropped under 2 seconds, farmer harvest yields grew 4x from 8kg to 35kg/box, and verified zero WCAG AA accessibility violations).

## 2. The Named Next Real Piece of Work

* **Project Title:** **EcoHive LoRaWAN Hardware Gateway & USSD Farmer Alert Engine**
* **Executive Summary:** Designing the physical IoT receiver layer that bridges field sensor telemetry from solar-powered beehives in Baringo and Nakuru directly into an Africa's Talking USSD/SMS gateway. This allows pastoralist Kenyan beekeepers without smartphones or internet access to receive real-time Swahili text alerts for hive weight surges and swarming frequencies (450–500 Hz).
* **Technical Scope:** 
  - Microcontroller firmware (ESP32 + SX1276 LoRa transceiver).
  - Africa's Talking SMS API webhook handler in Node.js/Express.
  - Multi-lingual SMS parsing (English and Swahili prompt sets).
